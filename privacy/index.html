Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Birdy app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Fred Harrison (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not collect or store personal information itself when you download and use it. Registration is not required to use the Application.


Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Application does not collect or share personal information directly with third parties. However, third-party services such as Apple’s in-app purchase system and RevenueCat may process limited data required to manage subscriptions. This data is handled securely and is not accessible to the Service Provider.


What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.


Device Permissions and On-Device Processing

The Application requires access to certain device permissions, including Screen Time and notifications, in order to provide its functionality.


The Application does not access, transmit, or store detailed app usage data outside of your device. All Screen Time information remains private and is managed by Apple’s system frameworks.


Subscription status is verified securely through Apple’s in-app purchase system and RevenueCat. No payment details or personal identity information are accessible to the Service Provider.


If you choose to contact support via email, the Application may pre-fill certain non-personal technical information (such as device model, iOS version, app settings, and anonymous identifiers) to help diagnose issues. This information is only shared if you choose to send the email and is not collected or stored by the Service Provider.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. The Service Provider encourages all children to never submit any personally identifiable information through the Application and/or Services. The Service Provider encourage parents and legal guardians to monitor their children's Internet usage and to help enforce this Policy by instructing their children never to provide personally identifiable information through the Application and/or Services without their permission. You must also be at least 16 years of age to consent to the processing of your personally identifiable information in your country (in some countries we may allow your parent or guardian to do so on your behalf).


Security

The Service Provider is committed to safeguarding your information. The Application does not collect personal information directly, and any limited data processed by third-party services (such as Apple’s in-app purchase system and RevenueCat) is handled securely.
